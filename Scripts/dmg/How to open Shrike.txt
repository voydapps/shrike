Shrike @VERSION@ - how to install and open it
=============================================

1. INSTALL

   Drag "Shrike" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
   Then eject this disk: in Finder, right-click it and choose Eject.
   Always start Shrike from your Applications folder, not from this disk.


2. FIRST LAUNCH (one time only)

   Shrike is not notarized by Apple. So the first time you open it,
   macOS asks you to confirm that you trust it. The steps depend on
   your macOS version (Apple menu > About This Mac).

   macOS 26 (Tahoe)
     Open Shrike from Applications. macOS asks for your Mac's password
     to allow the app. Enter it. Shrike opens.
     If you see "Shrike Not Opened" instead, follow the macOS 15 steps.

   macOS 15 (Sequoia)
     a. Open Shrike from Applications. macOS says "Shrike Not Opened".
        Click Done.
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the
        "Security" part. Next to the line about Shrike, click
        "Open Anyway".
     c. Enter your Mac's password. Then click "Open Anyway" once more.

   macOS 14 (Sonoma)
     In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Shrike and choose
     Open. In the message that appears, click Open.

   After this, Shrike opens with a normal double-click.


3. ACCESS TO YOUR FOLDERS

   When you open pictures from Desktop, Documents, Downloads, a USB
   drive or a network drive, macOS asks once whether Shrike may read
   that place. Click Allow. macOS remembers it, also after you
   install a newer version of Shrike.


IF MACOS SAYS "SHRIKE IS DAMAGED AND CAN'T BE OPENED"

   The download is broken or was changed. Move Shrike to the Trash,
   download the DMG again, and compare its checksum with the one on
   the website. In Terminal:
       shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Shrike-@VERSION@.dmg


FOR ADVANCED USERS

   This Terminal command skips step 2:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Shrike.app
